# Store listing draft

Name: Docaville

Short description: Edit documents and PDFs. Turn speech and scans into editable text on your device.

## Description

Create, edit, convert, and review PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and images in one workspace.

Turn recordings into editable transcripts and document drafts. Dictate into Word, listen to documents, translate between English, German, French, and Spanish, and recognize text in scans. Prepare reusable business templates, compare document text, automate PDF workflows, and restore local drafts and saved versions.

Document and audio processing runs on your device. Models and OCR language packs download when first needed, then supported tools work offline using cached resources. No account is required.

Review recognition and translation output before use. Office compatibility varies with document complexity. PDF text replacement/covering creates a flattened copy. Text comparison does not preserve native Word tracked changes. Speaker labels are manual.
